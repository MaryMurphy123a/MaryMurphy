Welcome to my corner of the internet

This is a place for ideas, observations, and stories worth returning to. It is a personal notebook made public: part archive, part conversation, and part record of what I am learning.

Why this site exists

Good ideas become clearer when we put them into words. This site gives me room to explore questions, share useful lessons, and practice explaining complex things in a simple way.

What you'll find here

Occasional essays, field notes from projects, talks, slide decks, and reflections on the work of making ideas understandable.
