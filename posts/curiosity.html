Building a practice of curiosity

Curiosity is not a personality trait reserved for a few naturally inquisitive people. It is a practice: a habit of looking longer, asking one more question, and allowing a new answer to change the first one.

Notice more

Start with ordinary things. Keep a small list of details that interrupt the routine. Patterns begin to appear when we give attention somewhere to stay.

Ask better questions

Replace assumptions with open questions. Ask what, how, and why—not to win an argument, but to understand the shape of the problem before reaching for an answer.
